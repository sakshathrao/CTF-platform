REPEATING-KEY CIPHER / MESSAGE RECOVERY
FILED BY
Communications Review Unit
STATUS
OPEN
CASE BRIEF

An outgoing telegram was recovered from the North Pier telegraph office. The message is readable as text, but its letters have been transformed using a repeating-key cipher.

The operator's handover memo identifies the word used at the desk. Investigators believe the ciphertext can be decoded using that word and a standard classical cipher tool.

OBJECTIVE
Recover the operator's repeating key, identify the cipher, and decrypt the telegram to obtain the flag.

INVESTIGATION STEPS
  1. Read the operator memo and recover the station call-sign used as the repeating key.
  2. Identify the repeating-key cipher described by the examiner.
  3. Use a Vigenère decoder with the recovered key.
  4. Keep braces, underscores and digits unchanged, then submit the decoded flag.
