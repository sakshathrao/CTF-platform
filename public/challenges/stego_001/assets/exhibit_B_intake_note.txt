CASE FILE: ST-08
UNIT: DIGITAL EVIDENCE / IMAGE EXAMINATION
STATUS: OPEN

A CCTV still was recovered from the records storeroom after a sealed property case was found unattended.

The image looks ordinary when viewed normally. The previous examiner noted that the evidence was exported through an image-processing workflow that can preserve hidden information in pixel data.

A red property tag is visible on the worktable. The examiner's handwritten note says:

    "The warning colour is carrying more than the scene."

OBJECTIVE
Examine the image for concealed data, identify the channel carrying the hidden message, and recover the recovery marker.
