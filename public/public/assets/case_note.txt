CASE FILE: NET-04
UNIT: Network Incident Response
STATUS: OPEN

A small office gateway was imaged after an employee reported that an internal report had been copied out of the archive service.

The capture was taken from the employee workstation during the relevant period. It contains ordinary background traffic and several HTTP requests. Investigators believe the report was transferred in clear text.

The workstation address was recorded as 10.10.4.21. The archive gateway was 192.0.2.44.

OBJECTIVE
Identify the HTTP request that retrieves the shift report, reconstruct the corresponding server response, and recover the recovery token contained in the report.

HANDOUT
Exhibit A — gateway_capture.pcap

SUBMISSION
Submit the token in the CTF flag format: CTF{...}

You may use Wireshark or another packet-analysis tool. No live network scanning or connection to the captured addresses is necessary.
